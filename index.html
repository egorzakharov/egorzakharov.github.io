Egor Zakharov

I am a Staff Research Scientist at Meta Reality Labs in Pittsburgh, where I lead a five-person research pod working on human-centric 4D reconstruction.

I received my Ph.D. from Skoltech, under the supervision of Victor Lempitsky. During my Ph.D., I also was a Lead Research Scientist at Samsung AI Center. After that, I did a Postdoc at ETH Zurich AIT lab
